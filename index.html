    Comprehensive Diabetic Care Plan & Precautions

    Clinical action steps, nutritional guidelines, exercise routines, and monitoring protocols.

    1. Immediate Medical Actions

    • Schedule Endocrinologist Consult: Book a clinical evaluation with a qualified physician for comprehensive diagnostic confirmation.
    • Laboratory HbA1c Blood Test: Undergo Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c) testing to measure average blood sugar levels over the past 3 months.
    • Fasting Blood Glucose Re-test: Perform an 8-hour fasting plasma glucose test to verify initial screening indicators.

    2. Dietary & Nutrition Plan

    • Low Glycemic Index Foods: Prioritize whole grains, oats, non-starchy vegetables, and legumes to avoid blood glucose spikes.
    • Carbohydrate Control: Limit refined sugars, sweetened beverages, white bread, and processed snack foods.
    • Fiber Intake: Consume at least 25–30g of soluble fiber daily to slow carbohydrate digestion and sugar absorption.

    3. Physical Activity & Routine

    • 150 Mins/Week Aerobic Activity: Engage in 30 minutes of moderate brisk walking or cycling 5 days a week.
    • Post-Meal Walking: Take a 10–15 minute walk after meals to increase insulin sensitivity and glucose clearance.
    • Resistance Training: Perform light strength training twice weekly to enhance muscle glucose uptake.

    4. Daily Glucose Monitoring

    • Fasting Blood Glucose Target: Aim for fasting levels between 80 – 130 mg/dL.
    • Post-Prandial Target: Keep blood glucose under 180 mg/dL 2 hours after meals.
    • Maintain Logbook: Track daily blood sugar readings, meals, and physical activity for physician review.

    5. Emergency Warning Signs & Hypoglycemia Management

    Low Blood Sugar (Hypoglycemia < 70 mg/dL)

    Symptoms: Shakiness, sweating, dizziness, rapid heartbeat, confusion.

    Action (15-15 Rule): Consume 15g fast-acting carbohydrates (e.g. 4 oz fruit juice or glucose tablet), wait 15 mins, and retest.

    High Blood Sugar (Hyperglycemia > 250 mg/dL)

    Symptoms: Extreme thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision, fruity breath odor.

    Action: Drink water, avoid carbohydrates, and seek urgent medical guidance if ketones are present.
